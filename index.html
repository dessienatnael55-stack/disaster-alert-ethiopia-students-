🛡️ Safety Tips

Before a Disaster

  • Prepare an emergency kit.
  • Know safe places and evacuation routes.
  • Follow trusted weather and safety updates.

During a Disaster

  • Stay calm and follow official instructions.
  • Move away from immediate danger.
  • Never walk or drive through floodwater.

After a Disaster

  • Avoid damaged buildings and fallen power lines.
  • Use safe drinking water.
  • Help others when it is safe to do so.
